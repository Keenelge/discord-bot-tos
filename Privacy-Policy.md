# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 16, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord bots developed and maintained by **vishnya_net_chereshnya** (the "Developer", "we", "us") collect, use, and store data. These bots are referred to as the "Bots". By using any of the Bots, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Data We Collect
Each Bot collects only the data it needs to provide its features. Depending on the Bot, this may include:
- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs;
- **Public profile information:** usernames, display names, and avatars, as provided by Discord;
- **Command data:** the commands you use and any options or text you enter in them;
- **Configuration data:** settings that server administrators choose for a Bot;
- **Feature-related data:** data created through a Bot's features, such as game progress, statistics, or records.

The Bots do not collect passwords, payment information, or private messages that are not addressed to them.

## 2. How We Use Data
We use this data only to:
- provide, operate, and maintain the Bots' features;
- store server settings and feature data;
- prevent abuse and enforce our [Terms of Service](LINK);
- fix bugs and improve the Bots.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share data only:
- with Discord, as part of normal operation through the Discord API;
- with hosting or infrastructure providers, only as needed to run the Bots;
- when the law requires us to.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
Data is stored on secured servers and kept only as long as a Bot needs it to provide its features. When a Bot is removed from a server, data related to that server may be deleted. Some data, such as records needed to prevent abuse, may be kept longer.

## 5. Your Rights
You can ask us to access or delete the data the Bots store about you, or about a server you administer. To make a request, contact the Developer (see Section 8). Deleting your data may mean some features no longer work for you.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Bots are intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from users below that age.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time. A new version takes effect when it is published. If you continue to use the Bots after a change, you accept the updated Policy.

## 8. Contact
If you have questions or data requests, contact the Developer on Discord: **vishnya_net_chereshnya**.
